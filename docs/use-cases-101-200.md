## Safety and scope requirements

- Never silently smooth, clamp, erase or quarantine safety-critical measurements where that could conceal a genuine hazard. Preserve raw readings and raise uncertainty or independent alarms.
- Trusted policies, roles, tool allowlists, paths, cryptographic trust anchors, sensor calibration and physical safety interlocks must come from authoritative configuration, not caller prompts.
- Specialized DSP, computer vision, scientific, clinical, avionics and industrial validation require separately qualified adapters and datasets; general text/schema checks do not establish physical or clinical correctness.
- For UC110, domain allowlists are trusted server-side; UC118 uses bounded synchronization tolerance; UC128 allows expected phase differences; UC132 verifies proofs cryptographically; UC140 uses isolated PDF parsing; UC150 does not classify emotional formatting as an attack.
- UC153, UC156, UC166, UC176, UC177, UC183 and UC189 require independent safety paths and no automatic suppression of alarms or actuation on unverified corrections.
- Each case needs fixture provenance, configuration, positive/negative/edge tests, false-positive review, timeouts, fail-closed or fail-review behavior and evidence of end-to-end enforcement before status can change.

## Architecture — proposed target

Customer application / agent / sensor pipeline → **Valinator policy enforcement and orchestration** → allow / quarantine / block / human review → downstream AI or tool. Four core services: (1) bounded schema and data-contract validation; (2) trusted versioned policy and authorization evaluation; (3) isolated specialist validator adapters with explicit external-egress gates; (4) redacted, tenant-scoped decision/evidence logging. Existing checks remain in place; no implicit execution or silent transformations. ISO/IEC 42001 support means evidence for an organization's AI management system, **not** automatic certification or compliance.

## Delivery phases

1. **P0:** UC101–110 tool policy, authorization, budgets, execution-state and downstream response trust boundaries; implement at actual enforcement layer.
2. **P1:** UC131–140 selective structural/cryptographic checks and UC171–180 regulated workflows, gated on reviewed specialist integrations.
